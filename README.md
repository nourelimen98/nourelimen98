## 🌟 Featured Project: MiMotion

**MiMotion — Technology Meets Music**

MiMotion explores the intersection of music, human interaction, and intelligent technology. Initially developed as my final-year engineering project, it combines a smart wearable musical glove with a digital learning platform.

The project explores wearable technology and gesture recognition, embedded systems and wireless communication, interactive music learning, and intelligent musical interaction.

My vision is to evolve MiMotion into a comprehensive technology platform for musicians, combining AI, voice recognition, computer vision, IoT, and immersive experiences.
